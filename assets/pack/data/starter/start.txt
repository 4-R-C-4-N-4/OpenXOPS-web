Mission: STARTER COMPOUND

Scouts spotted a small walled compound south of
the river, used as a supply point by hostiles.

Move in through the south gate, clear the yard
and the building, and secure the inner room.

Pick up the sidearm near the gate, or the SMG
inside the building's east room.

Guards patrol the courtyard, the watchtower by
the gate, and the building interior.

Objective: eliminate all hostiles and reach the
marker in the building's inner room.
