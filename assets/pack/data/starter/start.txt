Mission: STARTER COMPOUND

Hostiles hold a small walled compound used
as a supply point. You start inside the south
wall, behind cover.

Clear the yard and the building, and secure
the inner room. A spare sidearm lies beside
you; an M4 rifle waits in the east room.

Objective: eliminate all hostiles.
Select an empty slot (1/2) to pick up weapons.
