# Mission spec: "UFO ESCAPE" (stealth start, winding ship escape)

Status: approved idea from the user; implementation in progress.
Engine background: docs/ARCHITECTURE.md. Sister mission: docs/MISSION-UFO-FIELD.md
(reuses its aliens, lasers and wave/staging machinery).

## 1. Concept

The rancher from UFO FIELD wakes up in a holding cell aboard the alien ship.
He has nothing but a MOON ROCK he grabbed during the abduction. A guard stands
in the corridor outside, back turned. One clean headshot with the thrown rock
drops him, and his laser pistol becomes the way out. From there the player
fights through a twisting ship (cell block, maintenance crawlway, specimen
lab full of goo vats and pickled specimens, goo-processing hall, a switchback
ramp, the bridge) to the escape-pod bay. Reaching the pod wins.

This must NOT be another arena. Progress is a route through distinct spaces,
with short sight lines, corners, two floor levels and cover at every entrance.

## 2. Player-facing requirements

| # | Requirement |
|---|---|
| R1 | Start unarmed except a single throwable MOON ROCK (one throw, no reserve). |
| R2 | A clean rock headshot kills the first guard; a body hit does not. His ZAP PISTOL drops and can be picked up (empty slot 2, or slot 1 once the rock is thrown, since the thrown rock leaves the hand). |
| R3 | A winding, extensive ship: at least 7 distinct spaces, two decks joined by a ramp, doglegged corridors, no straight unobstructed line longer than ~160 units except one deliberately long hall that is broken up by staggered cover. |
| R4 | Cool art: glowing goo vats, specimen tubes with floating experiments, lab wall panels, light strips, hazard stripes, consoles, bulkheads. |
| R5 | Events: an alarm message and reinforcements when the player enters the lab; a final ambush in the pod bay; reaching the escape pod completes the mission. |
| R6 | Listed in the main mission list (after UFO FIELD), not ADD-ON. |
| R7 | Classic data unaffected; UFO FIELD unaffected except the laser-crosshair fix (§3 E9). |
| R8 | Difficulty fair for a long mission with no healing: measured with the real-engine pressure probe before release. |

## 3. Engine changes (parent-owned; additive)

### E7. Throwaway weapons
New `WeaponParameter.throwaway` (default false). For a throwaway weapon:
- spawned with only its loaded magazine (no TOTAL_WEAPON_AUTOBULLET reserve);
- when the last round is fired the weapon disables itself and frees the slot
  (same path the original uses for the grenade), so the hand is empty.

### E8. MOON ROCK (weapon 27) and bullet type 5
| id | name | hold template | atk | pen | rate | speed | mag | spread | bolt | sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 27 | MOON ROCK | M92F (3) pistol hold | 45 | 0 | 20 | 6 (slow, visible) | 1, throwaway | 0-2 | 5 rock | throw.wav (id 33) |

Damage check: alien drone HP 80. Headshot = 45 x 2 + rand(16) = 90..105, so it
kills. Body hit = 45 + rand(8) <= 52 and leg hit about 31: neither kills.
Bullet type 5 uses data/model/weapon/rock.x + rock.bmp at a small size.
TOTAL_PARAMETERINFO_WEAPON 27 -> 28, TOTAL_PARAMETERINFO_BULLET 5 -> 6.

### E8b. Captive rancher (human 47)
Same skin as type 43 (rancher.bmp), Weapon[0] = 27, Weapon[1] = none. HP tuned
by the pressure probe (start: 200). TOTAL_PARAMETERINFO_HUMAN 47 -> 48.

### E9. Laser crosshair fix (UFO FIELD feedback)
The player's own laser bolts fly straight down the line of sight. They are
drawn end-on, as a coloured blob sitting on the crosshair for their whole
flight. Classic bullets are tiny, so the original never showed this. Fix: in
first-person view, do not draw the player's own *laser* bolts (types 2-4).
Classic bullets are still drawn. Enemy bolts stay visible, and impacts and
sounds are unchanged.

### E10. Mission listing
`data/missions.txt` gains `UFO ESCAPE|...|data\ufoescape\|escape.mif|0|0`
after UFO FIELD (MIF-backed main-list entries are already supported).

## 4. Asset contract (assets worker)

All clean-room, deterministic, CC0, generated by `tools/escapegen/` (new
package, may import tools/ufogen, tools/modelgen and tools/artgen helpers).
Same formats as UFO FIELD: map textures are 256x256 8-bit BMP (BGR0 palette),
seamless where they tile. Models are engine-valid `.x` text via
tools/modelgen/xwriter.

Map textures (exactly these 10 names, `assets/pack/data/ufoescape/`):

| file | look | used for |

| floor.bmp | dark metal floor grating with cyan sub-glow | floors, ramps, catwalk |

| goo.bmp | bright radioactive green goo, bubbles, swirl (top surfaces) | vat tops, troughs |

| bulkhead.bmp | heavy door/bulkhead frame, orange warning lights | doorways, pod |

Models and sounds:
- `data/model/weapon/rock.x` + `rock.bmp` (128x128): irregular grey-blue
  moon rock with craters, <= 60 polygons, sized like a pistol (follow the
  M92F grip/muzzle anchors in docs/ASSET-CONTRACT.md §6.2). Also used as the
  thrown projectile (bullet type 5) at small scale.
- `data/model/tube.x` + `tube.bmp` (128x128): specimen tube prop,
  upright glass cylinder (8-12 sides) with a metal base and cap; texture shows
  green liquid and a floating specimen (a small cow on one side, an alien
  embryo on the other). Height about 26 world units at SMALLOBJECT_SCALE 5
  (file height about 5.2), radius about 5 world units.
- `data/sound/throw.wav` (whoosh), `data/sound/glass.wav` (glass shatter +
  liquid splash). Synthesize them with tools/sfxgen like tools/ufogen/sounds.py.
- Previews in `docs/img/escape/`. Tests in `tools/tests/test_escape_assets.py`
  (formats, sizes, determinism, polygon limits, palette, tube height).
- Provenance: write records to `PROVENANCE-ADD.jsonl` at the worktree root
  (NOT assets/provenance.jsonl). Each record: path, sha256, license CC0-1.0,
  author, generator, date, curated_by null, prompt/recipe, source_url.

## 5. Map contract (map worker)

Authoring: `tools/mapgen/examples/ufo_escape.py` builds the layout
programmatically and writes `tools/mapgen/examples/ufo_escape.json`.
`python3 -m tools.mapgen build ... -o assets/pack/data/ufoescape/ --pd1-name escape.pd1`
then produces `temp.bd1` + `escape.pd1`.

Hard limits: <= 160 blocks, <= 256 points, <= 10 textures (the §4 names),
<= 16 messages. Player height 20, collision radius 2.8, jump < 24.

### 5.1 Layout (world units; x east, z south, y up)

Two decks: lower floor y = 0 with ceiling 40; upper floor y = 44 with ceiling
88. Corridors 24-28 wide. Doorways 18-24 wide. Every space is enclosed: walls
on all sides and a ceiling over the whole walkable area. A suggested plan
follows; adjust coordinates freely, but keep the sequence and the design
rules in §5.2.

1. **Cell block** (lower, south-west). An N-S corridor with three cells on
   its west side. The player spawns in the southern cell facing its doorway.
   Guard 1 (drone, ZAP) stands in the corridor about 25-35 units from the
   player's doorway with his BACK to it. He does not patrol (AI wait path
   that faces away). The second cell holds a specimen tube; the third is
   empty. The north end turns into:
2. **Maintenance crawlway**: a corridor with at least two 90° doglegs, low
   pipes along one wall (non-blocking trim), one patrolling drone.
3. **Specimen lab** (lower, large: about 180x140). 3-4 octagonal goo vats
   (built from 3 raw blocks each: a centre box plus two trapezoid halves,
   with vat.bmp sides and goo.bmp tops, about 26 tall) arranged staggered so
   no aisle runs straight through. 2 operating tables, 6+ specimen-tube
   props along the walls, labpanel walls. A catwalk around two walls at y 24
   with a ramp, so the room has height and overlooks. The entrance faces a
   vat (cover within 30 units). On first entry: ALARM message +
   reinforcements (staged group 1) from the far door.
4. **Goo processing hall** (lower, long: about 200x60). The one long space.
   Break it with staggered pipe pillars and low goo troughs (12 tall, goo
   tops) so that the longest clear line is short and the player hops cover.
5. **Switchback ramp**: two ramps (raw sloped blocks, hazard.bmp edges) and
   a landing climb from y 0 to y 44. Hand-rails as thin blocks.
6. **Bridge** (upper): consoles (console.bmp) in a ring around a central
   holo-pillar, a raised captain's dais, long "window" walls. Brutes here.
7. **Escape-pod bay** (upper): pod capsules (bulkhead.bmp / hazard.bmp)
   along the far wall. On entry: ambush (staged group 2, brutes) from side
   hatches. Reaching the marked pod completes the mission.

### 5.2 Design rules (tested where possible)

- Sight lines: corridors change direction at most every ~80 units; apart from
  the processing hall, no straight unobstructed horizontal line at eye height
  (y 16) longer than about 160 units. The processing hall must have cover
  breaking any line longer than about 70 units.
- Every doorway has cover (wall corner, vat, console, trough) within about 30
  units on the player's side.
- Enemies are placed off-axis from doorways, so the player sees a room
  before the room shoots back. No enemy has a line of sight to the player's
  spawn cell.
- Fully enclosed: a perimeter and ceiling test proves there is no gap to the
  void, and a walkability flood-fill (coarse grid, using block AABBs at
  player height plus ramps) proves spawn -> pod is reachable.

### 5.3 Points and events

- Player spawn (type 1, human_type 47, p4 = 0).
- Human placement: about 14-16 placed aliens plus staged groups 1 (2 drones
  at the lab) and 2 (2 brutes at the pod bay). Use types 44/45/46, mostly 44
  and 45 early, 46 on the bridge and in the pod bay. Patrol paths use unique
  waypoint ids in a reserved range (e.g. 200-399). Never use ids -100,
  -110, -120: those are the engine's auto-started event entry points.
- Weapon pickups: a PHOTON SMG (25) in the lab and a PLASMA RIFLE (26) on the
  bridge.
- Specimen tubes: small-object points with the mission's addon prop (id 12).
  Addon file `data/ufoescape/tube.txt` (6 lines: model, texture, decide,
  hp <= 127, sound glass.wav, jump 0) plus `tube.txt.cover`
  (`0 halfwidth miny maxy halflength` world-unit body hull, see UFO FIELD).
- Event line -100: wait 2 s -> message 0 (WAKE UP: grab the rock, headshot the
  guard) -> arrival wait (event 13) at the lab entrance for the player (p2 =
  player p4 0) -> message 1 ALARM -> deploy group 1 (41) -> arrival wait at
  the pod-bay entrance -> message 2 -> deploy group 2 -> arrival wait at the
  escape pod -> complete (event 10). Read src/event.cpp before encoding the
  p1/p2/p3/p4 fields.
- `data/ufoescape/escape.mif`: name `UFO ESCAPE`, fullname at most 35 chars,
  map/points paths, sky 0, flags 0, addon `data\ufoescape\tube.txt`, picture
  `data\briefing\ufoescape.bmp`, briefing at most 14 lines x 52 chars (mention
  the rock headshot, empty hand, pickups, and the pod).
- `data/ufoescape/escape.msg` (<= 71 chars per line).
- Briefing picture generated from the map JSON (extend
  tools/artgen/briefing_pictures.py with `generate_ufoescape`).
- Tests `tools/tests/test_ufo_escape.py`: deterministic build, limits,
  perimeter/ceiling enclosure, walkability flood-fill spawn -> pod, sight-line
  sampling per §5.2, unique path ids that resolve only to waypoints, event
  chain order, mif/msg/tube.txt formats.
